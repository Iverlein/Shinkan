Shinkan holds an Android device for a child. Made the device owner once, by one adb command on a freshly set-up device, it decides which apps exist for the child, at which hours, and what the device may do.

On the closed device Shinkan is the child's whole home: two doors, a browser and a YouTube player, both Shinkan's own and filtered, each at the hours set and with its own volume ceiling; everything else is suspended. The home is never behind the lock screen. On the open device the child keeps the launcher and the parent closes the apps named, at the hours named. Installing, uninstalling, adding accounts, changing the clock, safe boot, factory reset from Settings and USB debugging are blocked.

The device's traffic goes through Shinkan's own filter, an always-on VPN with lockdown the child cannot switch off; ads and trackers are blocked from a bundled list, gambling, porn and social sites by choice. Share any link from any app to "Block in Shinkan" and the site is blocked device-wide, with every subdomain and the platform's other hosts, so the app goes dark too. Share a YouTube channel's page or one of its videos and the built-in player hides that creator everywhere while the official app and site are held; the player skips sponsor segments with SponsorBlock. The player has a block list or an allow list of creators, with Home, Creators and a History that resumes where a video stopped. The browser is Shinkan's own page with EasyList and EasyPrivacy. A day plan gives free hours, then budgets of minutes per door with rests between; in a rest playback stops and the child sees a ring closing until it ends, or until a parent opens the device.

The parent opens the settings with a PIN that exists only on the device and can release the device at any time. The launcher icon is the parent's choice among four drawn from the name: a crest, a gate, a drawn gate, a seal. No account, no server, no companion app. The filter forwards the device's own traffic and nothing of Shinkan's; nothing about the child leaves the device.
