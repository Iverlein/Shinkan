Shinkan holds an Android device for a child. Made the device owner once, by one adb command on a freshly set-up device, it decides which apps exist for the child, at which hours, and what the device may do.

Only the apps the parent allowed can be opened, each within the hours set; everything else is suspended. Installing, uninstalling, adding accounts, changing the clock, safe boot, factory reset from Settings and USB debugging are blocked.

The web is handled by the platform, not by the app: no browser unless allowed, a filtering DNS resolver of the parent's choice, and a site allow-list through the browser's own managed configuration. Shinkan never sees the traffic.

The parent opens the settings with a PIN that exists only on the device and can release the device at any time. No account, no server, no companion app, no INTERNET permission; nothing about the child leaves the device.
